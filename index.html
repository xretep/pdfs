Peter Xu

UCLA Hedrick

I am primarily interested in polylogarithms and Eisenstein cohomology, and their connections to motives, K-theory, and arithmetic generally: in particular, I like phenomena where combinatorial or geometric aspects of polylogarithms can be applied to understand L-values, units, cycles, or other concrete objects. I also have an interest in the p-adic Kudla program, as well as connections between topology and arithmetic.
