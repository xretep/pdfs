Peter Xu

peterx@math.ucla.edu

I am primarily interested in polylogarithms and Eisenstein cohomology, and their connections to motives, K-theory, and arithmetic generally: in particular, I like phenomena where combinatorial or geometric aspects of polylogarithms and related objects can be applied to understand concrete arithmetic information like L-values, cycles, or (especially) units. I also have an interest in the p-adic Kudla program, as well as connections between topology and arithmetic.
